Water the field only when it's thirsty.

AgriFlow connects soil-moisture and climate sensors to an ESP8266 microcontroller that switches the water pump automatically and reports everything to a live dashboard, which farmers can also override remotely.

Features

Built for the field

  • Temperature and humidity telemetry
  • Auto pump control on a user-defined threshold
  • Instant manual override from phone or desktop
  • Radial gauge, status badges and an activity log
