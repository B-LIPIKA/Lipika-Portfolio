The problem

Simple baselines miss the shape of the day

Repeat-last-day and moving-average forecasts can't capture overlapping daily cycles or non-linear process shifts, so operators get late warnings when inflow jumps.

The approach

A tiny foundation model, fine-tuned

  • Chronological 70 / 15 / 15 split, so no future data leaks into training
  • 192-step context window, 96-step forecast horizon
  • Multivariate inputs: inflow, pressure and turbidity
  • Automated error analysis for normal vs. surge regimes
